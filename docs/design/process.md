# Engagement process: koivupiilo.fi

Run by the agent as the studio lead. Each phase ends at a **gate**: Jarkko signs off a deliverable before the
next phase starts. No visual design before the strategy gate.

| # | Phase | What happens | Deliverable | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Discovery** | Desk research (web presence, jlaine.net, competitors, Finnish search, the volleyball landscape); stakeholder interview in 3 short blocks; audit of existing content and assets (photos, product screens) | `discovery.md`: findings, the problem in one paragraph, goals and measures | Jarkko: "this is the right problem" |
| 2 | **Strategy** | Audiences and their jobs; message hierarchy in Jarkko's words (FI first); site structure (front / Studio / Valmennus / products) and the role of jlaine.net; content plan; success measures | `strategy.md` + sitemap + content inventory (who writes what) | Jarkko signs the message and the sitemap |
| 3 | **Concept** | Two visual territories as boards: references at the bar (Werklig, Koto, Panic), Jarkko's grove photos, type pairings, the bark element, motion notes. No pages yet | Two concept boards | Jarkko picks one |
| 4 | **Design** | Front page and Magistician page, phone + desktop, light + dark; motion spec; outside critic against the bar before Jarkko sees anything | Comps + screen recording | Jarkko approves |
| 5 | **Build and content** | Bridgetown, FI/EN parity, performance and accessibility budgets, cookie-free page counts | Working site on a preview URL | Jarkko approves |
| 6 | **Launch and learn** | DNS to GitHub Pages; review after 30 days against the measures | Launch + 30-day note | — |

Rules: clarity wins (positioning.md); Finnish copy starts from Jarkko's words; every claim verified;
the agent pushes back where the evidence points elsewhere and records decisions in `model.md`.
