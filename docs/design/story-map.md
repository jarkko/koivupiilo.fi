(J), walkthroughs (W) and decisions (D) in `brief.md` and `model.md`; they do
not restate the model.

## 🧑 Pick the first slice

- **A (recommended): R1 as above.** Four full product pages plus short entries
  for the other four. Proves the "studio first" framing at launch.
  Tradeoff: a few more days of writing; Jarkko reviews four case texts.
- **B: minimum.** Front, services, contact, about, and *one* full product
  page (Magistician); the others as one-paragraph entries. Ships fastest.
  Tradeoff: the "studio first" claim rests on a single case.

Either is fully reversible; B grows into A without structural change.
