## Why this round happened

Four rounds of visual comps were rejected as generic. The root cause was not styling. The brief tried to say
three unrelated things on one front page (software, volleyball coaching, leadership), so no headline could be
both true and sharp, and the visuals had no idea to carry. Fix the message first, then design.

## The story (the concept)

Koivupiilo is a real place: the birch grove in Kangasala that Jarkko's children named, where the family house
was later built. The grove photo in comp B (IMG_1327, September 2013) is that spot. There are about 30 of
Jarkko's own photos of the same grove from 2013–2025 across every season.

- The birch is not decoration; it is the address. Trunk pattern, light and seasons all come from this one spot.
- "Piilo" (hideout) is the positioning: one person making things in a quiet place, away from agency noise.
- Telling it: a short line in Jarkko's own words. Children are not named or shown; the house is not shown.

- Grouped by Jarkko's two crafts (building software, coaching people), not by audience. *Valmennus* covers sport
  and workplace coaching in Finnish without strain.
- Clubs sit on both sides (Kvitti and a coaching clinic). Each side links to the other where it helps.
- Names: "Studio", singular (one person, no "we"). Not "Studios" (reads as a game company) and not "consulting"
  (says nothing).

## Rules for design

1. **Clarity wins.** A warm lead on a phone sees within 10 seconds who this is, what he makes and how to reach
   him. If a design idea gets in the way of that, the idea loses.
2. **Craft shows in execution, not effects**: exact type, real screens, speed, motion that means something,
   a Magistician page good enough to send on its own. Craft weighs most on Studio and the product pages.
3. **The site follows the season.** The front-page grove photo matches the current season in Kangasala
   (same spot, different year). One quiet, ownable detail; no other gimmicks needed.
4. **Finnish first, in Jarkko's voice.** Lines that carry the voice come from Jarkko; the agent fits the rest
   and derives the English. No meta-talk on the page ("kerron…", "tässä on…").
5. **The bar:** Werklig and Koto for visual exactness; Panic for saying it all in one sentence. Every comp is
   judged side by side with them; anything that doesn't hold up is not done.

## Appetite (replaces "small")

The site is both the business card and proof of craft. First: one excellent front page and one excellent
product page (Magistician), FI+EN, at full finish. Studio and Valmennus landing pages next. Everything else
after. Quality before quantity.
