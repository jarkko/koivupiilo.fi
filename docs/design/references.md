## Ours must beat X at Y

**First 10 seconds.**
- Beat **Panic** at the one-sentence definition: Koivupiilo's products and offers in one breath, in Finnish first, with no adjectives.
- Beat **CSS Wizardry** at showing proof above the fold: real product screens (not logos) on the front page.

**Services.**
- Beat **Kisko Labs** at stage-shaped offers: three plain offers, each with who it's for, how it starts and the format. A price floor only where Jarkko confirms one (open question for R1).
- Beat **Set Studio** at turning "one person" into the reason: you talk to the person who builds it, and he tells you honestly when he has no capacity (D4).

**Case study.**
- Beat **thoughtbot** at outcome-first: a header strip (what, for whom, role, stack, years, **status**), the problem in one sentence, the hard part, real screens; under 700 words; full parity FI/EN.
- Beat **CSS Wizardry** at candour: every case states its honest status (prototype / in use / live) and what is not done yet (M1).

**Contact.**
- Beat **Clearleft** at the named human: Jarkko's own address on every page, his face or at least his name.
- Beat **everyone** at expectations: "what to include" (three things), reply time, current availability, languages. Nobody in the set does this.

**Cross-cutting gaps nobody fills** (cheap ways to beat the whole set):
system dark mode honoured properly; full FI/EN parity with translated slugs
and `hreflang` pairs; a reply-time promise.
