## Solutions

<!-- Candidate solutions, each under the opportunity it serves. Several per opportunity; compare before building. -->

- **O-c1, O-c2 → 2x (critique `5c2e92ac`): first paint is the final type.** Gate each subset by unicode-range (the defect review's F1). Then preload PT Serif 400 latin and PT Sans 700 latin, and add metric-matched Georgia/Arial fallbacks (size-adjust, ascent/descent-override) so the swap moves nothing. Counter-argument: preload competes with the CSS on slow links, and the unicode-range fix alone gets most of the LCP gain. Measure both before keeping preload. Evidence: `docs/design/reviews/evidence/5c2e92ac/lh-compare.txt`, `cls-probe.json`.
- **O-c1, O-c2 → 2x (critique `a3bbe0b1`): stable first paint without waiting for fonts.** Drop the three preloads and ship metric-matched Georgia/Arial fallbacks (size-adjust, ascent/descent/line-gap-override from PT Serif 400 and PT Sans 400/700), so text paints at about 372 ms and the swap barely moves it at either link speed. Counter-argument: size-adjust matches average width, so the 390 px nav can still flip its 'SUOMEKSI' wrap; and HTTP/2 prioritisation in production may remove the preload penalty. Pass line, measured on production: FCP within 100 ms of no-preload and CLS under 0.05 at 1.6 Mbps and 400 kbit/s. Evidence: `docs/design/reviews/evidence/a3bbe0b1/fcp-probe.json`, `lh-devtools-ab.json`.

- **O-c3 → 2x (critique `405a839d`):** One authorized real product capture in the front product block, responsive and identically accessible in both languages, with synthetic data identified where needed. Counter-argument: D14's short company-site remit and the privacy, loading and maintenance cost. Compare the time to the trust moment before committing to the imagery; no Finnish wording change is part of this proposal. Full evidence: `.ds/reviews/405a839da7dda29b8b26f2ebdd395ba31ad4b1aff05c34952b2d75ea6be2adfe.critique.json`.
- **S-r1: O-r2 → single 2x candidate (critique `eef7d25e`, W0): replace the passive hero with one responsive real-app click-preview proof panel.** Use actual native-window context and a readable preview/result in a compact static composition from the approved map. Keep the decided native-Mac-plus-pipeline positioning. This is a presentation proposal, with no brand/model/lifecycle change; any later expansion into such a change goes to the owner. Test target: halve the time to identify native Mac UI and correctly explain the shown click, preserving requirement and signup findability. Strongest counter-argument: a two-frame panel or shrunken window may destroy course/detail legibility and displace the first-screen field. Reject it if readable real proof cannot fit the existing proof area within web budgets. Evidence and full counter-argument: [critique `two_x`](../../.ds/reviews/eef7d25e8c6941de8e882619974b890fb73b53ec535d060b77af029feab7f054.critique.json). No conversion or 2x result is claimed.
