What works today

  • Map

    Open the map from a PDF, PNG, JPEG or TIFF file and enter its scale.

  • Shared controls

    Courses share controls. Move a control and it moves on every course that uses it.

  • Control descriptions

    Ratametsuri draws the control descriptions in IOF 2024 symbols and updates them as you edit the course.

  • PDF and IOF XML

    Save the courses as a PDF for printing, each course on its own page with its control descriptions. Take them to your results software as an IOF XML 3.0 file.
