Concept 1 · Tuohi (birch bark)

Koıvupıılo

The birch becomes the company’s mark and system. The tittles of the three i’s are bark lenticels, a strip of your own trunk runs down the edge of every page, and the rest is exact type on bark-white. It reads as an identity a company owns, not as a website.

Why it fits the job

The site’s first job is the ten-second company check (strategy §1). A clear identity is what a checker reads as “established”: a mark that also sits in the product footers, on invoices and on the App Store seller page.

Nothing in it depends on unapproved photos. One grove photo appears on Yritys, and that’s all.

  • The mark travels. The lenticel i’s work at 16 px (favicon), in the “Koivupiilo Oy” credit on kvitti.fi and magistician.com, and at 11 rem.
  • The facts are a register. Name, Y-tunnus, Kangasala and email sit in a tabular strip at the foot of every page, in the same place every time.
  • The bark is real. The pattern is traced from your trunk photo (IMG_1327, the grove, 2013), not a stock texture.
  • Ages slowly. One family, one accent, no trend effects.

Hanken Grotesk only (OFL, self-hosted, variable 100–900). One family is the point: the system carries the identity, so type stays quiet and exact.

Finnish sets well in it: long compounds (rantalentopallokartta) hold together, and ä and ö keep open counters at small sizes.

Paid alternative worth one line in the bundled proposal: a grotesk with more drawing in it (e.g. from a Nordic foundry) for the wordmark only.

Bark-white and ink, with one accent: the rust-orange of the inside of birch bark (the side you see when you peel tuohi). It’s used only for focus rings and the active link. In dark mode the trunk turns light on near-black: same tree, at night.

Left half light, right half dark. Ratios are text on paper, WCAG AA in both schemes. They get re-checked with ds contrast once tokens exist.

The bark element

One trunk, used three ways and nowhere else: as the left edge of each page, as the lenticels in the mark, and as a faint field on the 404 page (“eksyit koivikkoon”).

It’s a vector traced from your photo, so it is sharp at any size, costs about 30 KB gzipped, and takes its colour from the theme. The trunk scrolls with the page, with no parallax.

Photography

One photo, one place. The grove appears on Yritys, beside the story of the name, in full colour and uncropped beyond the frame. The rest of the site is type and trunk, so the photo means something when it appears.

No product screenshots on this site (D14): the products’ own sites carry them.

Founder portrait: a plain, natural-light photo of you on Yritys. None exists yet, so it needs shooting.

Motion

  • Trunk grows, once. On first load per session, the edge trunk draws in top to bottom: 900 ms, ease-out, after 150 ms. Purpose: the one moment that says “this is a birch”. It doesn’t replay on navigation.
  • Links: underline thickness 1 → 2 px, 120 ms.
  • Nothing else moves. No scroll reveals, parallax or autoplay.
  • Reduced motion: the trunk is simply there.

References, at the bar

  • Werklig (Helsinki): an identity built from one rule applied everywhere, and exact grids. We take the discipline: the trunk and the lenticel are the only rule.
  • Koto: a brand mark drawn from something specific to the client, with type that stays out of its way. We take the specificity: the mark comes from this one tree.
  • Panic: the whole company in one sentence, above everything. We take the one sentence and the register strip.
