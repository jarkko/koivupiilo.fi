## Training Methodology

### 1. **Assessment-Driven Development**
Every athlete journey begins with comprehensive assessment of technical, tactical, physical, and mental capabilities.

### 2. **Constraints-Led Learning**
Instead of rigid drills, I design practice environments that encourage natural skill emergence and adaptation.

### 3. **Variability as a Feature**
Embracing variability in movement and decision-making to build robust, adaptable performance.

### 4. **Whole-Part-Whole Integration**
Maintaining game context while developing specific skills, ensuring transfer to competition.

### 5. **Athlete-Centered Communication**
Developing each athlete's ability to self-regulate, problem-solve, and take ownership of their development.
