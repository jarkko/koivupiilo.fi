## My Approach

### 1. **Authentic Self-Discovery**
We start by understanding your unique strengths, values, and communication style. No generic solutions—everything is tailored to who you are.

### 2. **Practical Application**
Every insight must translate to real-world action. We practice new approaches in low-risk environments before applying them to high-stakes situations.

### 3. **Systems Perspective**
Individual development happens within organizational and cultural contexts. We consider how your growth impacts and is impacted by your environment.

### 4. **Sustainable Change**
Quick fixes don't last. We build habits and practices that support long-term development and prevent regression under pressure.

### 5. **Measurement & Adjustment**
Regular feedback loops ensure we're making progress toward your goals and adapting the approach as you grow.
