## The Ratametsuri beta

You apply for the private Ratametsuri beta through a survey. If you apply, Koivupiilo stores your answers, your email address and your consent. The address gets a confirmation email, the beta invite and word of the app's release. The basis is your consent.

If you don't confirm your address, it is deleted within a week of the last confirmation email. A confirmed address is kept until you remove it. Every email has a link for removing your address. After that, your answers can't be traced back to you.

Koivupiilo also uses the answers to develop the app and deletes them after two years at the latest. If you answer without applying, your answers are anonymous, and their use is based on a legitimate interest in hearing from the app's future users.
