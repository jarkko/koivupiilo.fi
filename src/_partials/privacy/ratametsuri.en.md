## Ratametsuri beta invites

When you ask for a Ratametsuri beta invite, Koivupiilo stores your email address and your consent. The address gets only a confirmation email, the beta invite and word of the app's release. The basis is your consent.

If you don't confirm your address, it is deleted within a week of the last confirmation email. A confirmed address is kept until you remove it. Every email has a link for removing your address.

If you answer the Ratametsuri survey, Koivupiilo uses your answers to develop the app and deletes them after two years at the latest. The basis is a legitimate interest in hearing from the app's future users.
