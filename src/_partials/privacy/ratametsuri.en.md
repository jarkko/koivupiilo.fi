## Ratametsuri

Ratametsuri is an app by Koivupiilo Oy. This page explains what data we collect and how we handle it.

### Who is responsible

Koivupiilo Oy is the controller of your personal data. For anything about your data, write to <hello@join.koivupiilo.fi>.

### What we collect

When you sign up, we store your email address, what you signed up for and the record of your consent. Giving this data is voluntary and not a legal or contractual requirement. Without an email address, you can't sign up.

When you answer a survey, we store your answers. Giving your email address in a survey is optional. If you give it, we handle it like any other sign-up.

### What we use it for, and on what basis

We email you only what you agreed to:

- emails about your sign-up
- an invite to try the app before release
- news when the app is released

This is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the EU General Data Protection Regulation). You can withdraw it at any time with the “Remove me” link in our emails or by writing to us. Withdrawing doesn't affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

We use survey answers to develop the app, based on our legitimate interest in hearing from future users (Article 6(1)(f)).

Based on our legitimate interest, we also protect our forms from abuse and keep a record of removed addresses so that we don't email them again.

### Who handles your data

Service providers that run our server and send our emails handle your data on our behalf. We don't sell your data or give it to anyone else.

### How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up until you remove your address or ask us to remove it. If you don't confirm your sign-up, we delete it seven days after the last confirmation email. We delete the answers to “Survey for course setters” 24 months after they were given.

When you remove your address, we keep an identifier derived from it permanently so that we don't email you again.

### Your rights

You have the right to

- know what data we hold about you, and get a copy in a portable format
- ask us to correct data that is wrong
- ask us to delete your data
- ask us to restrict how we process it
- object to processing based on our legitimate interest
- withdraw your consent at any time.

To use these rights, write to <hello@join.koivupiilo.fi>.

If you think we process your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Finnish data protection authority (Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman): <https://tietosuoja.fi>.
