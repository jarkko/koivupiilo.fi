The Challenge: Build a social media advertising platform that could handle viral growth and complex targeting across multiple social networks during the early days of social advertising.

The Solution:

  • Architected scalable Rails backend handling millions of social interactions daily
  • Built real-time analytics dashboard for campaign optimization
  • Designed API integrations with Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube
  • Led team of 6 engineers through rapid scaling phase

The Result:

  • $350M acquisition by Google in 2012
  • Platform powered social advertising for Fortune 500 brands
  • Technology integrated into Google’s advertising infrastructure
  • Team and architecture sustained post-acquisition growth

Key Learning: Even in high-growth environments, human-centered development practices enable both technical excellence and team sustainability.

🏐 Championship Coaching Results

Finnish Beach Volleyball Championships

Role: Head Coach Timeline: 2015-Present Athletes: Regional and national level competitors

The Challenge: Develop athletes from recreational players to championship-level competitors using evidence-based training methods rather than traditional approaches.

The Methodology:

  • Applied nonlinear pedagogy and constraints-led coaching
  • Integrated motor learning research with practical training
  • Focused on decision-making under pressure rather than mechanical repetition
  • Emphasized athlete autonomy and self-regulation skills

The Results:

  • Multiple Finnish Championship medals across different age categories
  • Regional leadership role - Head Coach, Southwest Finland
  • Coach education impact - Trained 15+ coaches in modern methodology
  • Athlete retention - 90%+ continue playing after program completion

Key Learning: Modern pedagogical approaches accelerate skill acquisition while maintaining intrinsic motivation—principles that apply equally to athlete and software developer training.
