Course setting app for the Mac

You export your courses to your results software as an IOF XML 3.0 file, as you do from other course setting apps. The app is in development. Status checked 23 Nov 2026.

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • A map as a PDF, PNG, JPEG or TIFF file

What Ratametsuri can already do

  1. 1The pointer shows what a click will do

    Over another course's control, the text next to the pointer reads, for example, “Add 31 as 4”, and over a leg, “Insert between 2 and 3”. Click a control that is already on the course and the click selects it instead of adding it a second time.

  2. 2In Finnish

    The Finnish version uses the Finnish Orienteering Federation's terms, such as “rastin­määritteet” (control descriptions) and “rasti­pisteen kohde” (control feature).

  3. 3PDF and IOF XML 3.0

    Each course gets its own PDF with the control descriptions on the map. You can't edit columns E, G and H yet. MeOS 5.0 has imported Ratametsuri's courses without warnings. We haven't tried other results software.
