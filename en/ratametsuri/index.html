What Ratametsuri can already do

  1. 1See what a click will do before you click

    Next to the pointer you read, for example, “Add 31 as 4” or “Insert between 2 and 3”. If you hit a control that's already on the course, the click selects it and doesn't add it again.

  2. 2Courses straight to your results software

    MeOS reads them as they are.

  3. 3The control descriptions place themselves on the map

    If they don't fit the page in one column, a second one goes beside it. They're on the map in the course PDF too.
