Coaching

I coach volleyball and beach volleyball, train coaches and help new team leads get started.

Jarkko Laine, founder of Koivupiilo

What I coach

  • Volleyball and beach volleyball

    Coaching for clubs, teams and players.

  • Coach education

    Training for club coaches.

  • Coaching for new team leads

    For people who have just started leading a team.

I agree the content and scope with each club, team or person separately.

Background

  • Club official of the season 2025 (Kauden seuratoimija), Finnish Volleyball Association
  • Organiser of the Pyynikki beach volleyball tournaments
  • Finnish translation of the beach volleyball rules for 2025–2028 (in progress)

Software

Koivupiilo also builds software for volleyball. Magistician and the beach volleyball court map are two examples.
