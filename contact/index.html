Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with clients outside Finland?

Absolutely. Most of my technical consulting and development coaching happens remotely with clients across Europe, North America, and beyond. For sports coaching, I focus primarily on the Nordic region but have worked with athletes internationally.

What’s your typical engagement timeline?

  • Technical projects: 3-6 months for new development, 1-3 months for modernization
  • Coaching programs: 3-6 months for structured development
  • Advisory roles: Ongoing retainer relationships
  • Speaking/workshops: Single sessions or short-term engagements

How do you handle different time zones?

I regularly work with teams across multiple time zones. My schedule accommodates:

  • European hours (standard)
  • US East Coast overlap (afternoon meetings)
  • US West Coast (limited availability)
  • Asia Pacific (early morning meetings)

What information should I include in my initial email?

For technical projects:

  • Brief description of your current system/challenges
  • Technology preferences or requirements
  • Project timeline and budget range
  • Team size and structure

For coaching/development:

  • Current role and experience level
  • Specific areas where you want to grow
  • What’s driving the need for coaching now
  • Preferred format (individual, group, workshop)

Do you offer fixed-price projects?

For technical consulting: Yes, after thorough scope definition. Most clients prefer this for predictable budgeting.

For coaching/development: I offer both structured programs (fixed price) and ongoing coaching (monthly retainer).

For advisory roles: Typically monthly retainer or equity arrangements for startups.
