# Image and video credits (mockup A, placeholders)

All imagery is stock placeholder material for the style proposal. KVNN's own photos and video replace it. Photos were colour-graded slightly warmer and resized to WebP; the video was trimmed to 13 s, cropped and re-encoded without audio. Every image on the pages is a sample, labelled "Mallikuva / Sample photo" or "Mallivideo / Sample video" where it leads a section, and covered by the footer note.
