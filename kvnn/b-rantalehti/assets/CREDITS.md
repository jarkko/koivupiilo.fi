# Asset credits (mockup B, Rantalehti)

All imagery is free-licensed stock used as **placeholders** and labeled on the page as sample photos ("Esimerkkikuva" / "Sample photo"). KVNN's own photos and video replace it. No AI-generated imagery. Files come from the earlier B mockup's downloads (≤2400 px source, WebP).

How the files were made:

- `photos/*-800.webp` (grid) and `photos/*-1800.webp` (viewer): the stock photo, resized, in full colour. Only the gallery and the gallery teaser show colour.
- `riso/*-ht.webp`: the same photo (some cropped), greyscale, levels/gamma/sigmoidal contrast adjusted (some with a light base tint), converted to a 6 px ordered-dither halftone, 1-bit, lossless WebP. The page prints it in teal (dark mode: aqua ink on black stock, so the light parts of the photo glow).
- `riso/*-tone.webp`: the same crop in greyscale, used as the pink tint plate (dark mode: inverted and thresholded, so pink lands on the subject's shadows).
- `video/cover*.mp4`: the drone clip, trimmed to an 8 s cross-faded loop, H.264, no audio (1280×720 ≈1.1 MB, portrait 540×960 ≈0.63 MB). The page prints it into the teal cover with CSS.
- `video/cover-poster*.webp`: a frame of the clip, greyscale.
- Shop garments (tee, hoodie, cap) are inline SVG drawings made for the mockup, printed like the photos (teal plate, off-register pink plate, halftone shading), no source photo.
- `paper-grain.webp`: generated noise tile (ImageMagick), no source photo.
